01 — About the Campus

One campus. Every discipline robotics, drones and AI depend on.

NRDAI Campus unites academies, laboratories, manufacturing floors and competition arenas inside a single ecosystem — built so a student, a researcher and a founder can share the same corridor.

MISSION

Train the builders, not just the operators

To cultivate a generation of engineers, researchers and entrepreneurs who design, manufacture and deploy robotics, drone and AI systems — not merely study them from a textbook.

VISION

A regional hub the world builds with

To position Central Asia as a recognized center for applied robotics, drone technology and artificial intelligence — trusted by governments, universities and industry worldwide.

02 — Innovation Ecosystem

Nine functions under one roof, wired to work together.

From first lesson to first patent to first product shipped — the campus is built so ideas move between divisions, not around them.

03 — Academies

Seven academies. One shared foundation year.

Structured tracks from foundational electronics through advanced specialization — each academy feeding directly into the campus's research centers and startup hub.

04 — Research & Innovation Labs

Where academic research becomes a working prototype.

Twelve specialized laboratories and centers support everything from embedded systems to smart-city research, feeding manufacturing and the startup accelerator directly.

05 — Campus Zones & Arenas

Purpose-built ground for testing, training and competition.

Dedicated arenas and simulation zones for emergency response, agriculture, and competitive robotics — designed to mirror real operating conditions.

06 — Competitions

National qualifiers. International stage.

Eight competitive disciplines run year-round, culminating in an annual international invitational hosted on campus.

07 — Startup Hub

From prototype to funded company, without leaving campus.

The Incubation & Acceleration Program pairs early founders with mentors, laboratory access, manufacturing capacity, and a direct line to investors at Demo Day.
